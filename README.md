# 🏡 Tiny Town: your to-do list builds a village

Tiny Town is a cosy to-do list where every finished task grows a little village.

- 🌱 **Small task** → a potted plant appears
- 🏠 **Medium task** → a new shop opens
- 🏰 **Big task** → a new shop **and** a new animal neighbour moves in and walks around town

The town has **day and night** (it follows your real clock, or you can switch it), little **sparkle** animations when something is built, and it **remembers your town** even after you close the tab.

## ✨ Features
- Add tasks and choose their size (small / medium / big)
- Tick a task → something gets built, with a pop animation and sparkles
- 15 different shops, 34 plants and 14 animal neighbours to collect
- Neighbours wander around the town on their own
- Day / night mode with stars and a rising moon (auto, day or night)
- Rename your town
- Everything is saved in your browser (localStorage). No account needed
- Works on laptops and phones

## 🛠️ Built with
- **HTML**: the structure of the page
- **CSS**: colours, layout, animations (keyframes, transitions), CSS variables, responsive design
- **JavaScript**: the logic: saving data (localStorage + JSON), building the town, event listeners, timers

No frameworks or libraries: just the three basic web languages.

## 📁 Project files
```
index.html        → the page structure
style.css         → how everything looks
script.js         → how everything works
assets/
  background.jpg  → the meadow landscape
  shops/          → 15 shop images
  plants/         → 34 potted plant images
  villagers/      → 14 animal villager images
```

## 🚀 How to run it
- **Online:** open the GitHub Pages link above.
- **On your computer:** download the folder and open `index.html` in Chrome.

## 🔒 Privacy
Your tasks and town are saved **only in your own browser**. Nothing is sent anywhere.
(This also means clearing your browser data will reset your town.)
